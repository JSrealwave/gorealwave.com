# IVS Market Overview & Trends
**Intelligent Video Surveillance – 2026 Market Perspective**

### Executive Summary
The global video surveillance market has shifted from simple recording systems to intelligent, AI-driven platforms that deliver operational value beyond traditional security. 

As of 2025, the overall video surveillance market (hardware, software, and services) is valued at approximately **$83–84 billion**. Recent reports project strong growth of 11–13.5% CAGR, with the market exceeding **$100 billion by 2028** and reaching roughly **$140–160 billion by 2030**. 

The AI-enabled segment of the market is growing significantly faster, with CAGRs reported between 21% and 30.6%. This acceleration is driven by the move to edge AI, hybrid architectures, and the need for real-time operational intelligence rather than post-event review.

Key market drivers include the need to reduce false alarms, convergence of physical security with IT/OT, privacy regulations, and the desire to extract business value from existing camera investments. Organizations are increasingly looking for open, flexible platforms that can manage AI models across heterogeneous hardware while avoiding vendor lock-in.

### Market Size & Growth (2025–2031)
- **Overall Video Surveillance Market (hardware + software + services)**: ~$83–84 billion in 2025
  - Projected to exceed **$100 billion by 2028**
  - Expected to reach **$140–160 billion+ by 2030–2034** (Grand View Research, Fortune Business Insights, Precedence Research)
- **AI in Video Surveillance**: Fastest-growing segment, with CAGRs of 21–30.6% through 2030
- **Narrower equipment/software-only estimates** (e.g., MarketsandMarkets): $56.11 billion in 2025 → $88.06 billion by 2031 (CAGR 7.8%)

**Note on variance**: Different analyst firms use varying scopes. Broader definitions that include services, cloud/VSaaS, and AI analytics layers produce the higher figures commonly referenced in 2025–2026 reporting.

### Key Technology & Market Trends (2025–2026)
- **Intelligent Edge & Hybrid AI** — Processing is rapidly moving to the camera edge. On-device analytics reduce bandwidth, storage costs, and latency while improving privacy and enabling real-time decisioning.
- **AI Model Management** — Growing demand for platforms that can deploy, monitor, update, and optimize AI models across mixed hardware environments (Intel, AMD, ARM, NVIDIA Jetson, Hailo, etc.) with remote OTA updates.
- **False Alarm Reduction & Video Verification** — Modern AI video verification consistently achieves 70–95%+ reduction in false alarms. Traditional motion detection still generates ~97% false positives in many environments.
- **Video-Verified / Remote Video Monitoring (RVM)** — Becoming a standard requirement for enterprise and SLED customers seeking reliable, cost-effective monitoring without constant on-site guards.
- **PhySec + InfoSec Convergence** — Video surveillance is increasingly owned and budgeted by IT/CISO teams rather than facilities, accelerating the shift toward managed service providers.
- **Privacy-Preserving Analytics** — On-camera redaction and privacy-by-design are becoming table stakes, especially in education, healthcare, government, and regulated industries.
- **Operational Intelligence** — Video is being used for business outcomes (safety compliance, process optimization, customer experience) in addition to security.

### Industry Drivers & Challenges

**Drivers**
- Rising security threats and regulatory compliance requirements
- Labor shortages and high cost of manned guarding
- Need for faster, more reliable incident response and reduced alert fatigue
- Desire to monetize existing camera investments through operational insights
- Cloud repatriation and data sovereignty concerns pushing hybrid models

**Challenges**
- Alert fatigue from legacy motion-based systems
- Complexity of deploying and managing AI across heterogeneous hardware
- Data privacy, sovereignty, and compliance requirements
- Integration with legacy infrastructure and third-party systems

### Opportunities for Open, AI-Native IVS Platforms
Organizations are actively seeking platforms that combine:
- Strong core VMS/media engine performance across edge, server, and cloud
- A universal, hardware-agnostic AI pipeline with easy model deployment and remote OTA updates
- Deep integration capabilities (45,000+ devices supported) and open APIs
- Proven analytics that deliver measurable ROI (false alarm reduction, faster response times, operational efficiency)

Platforms built on **lightweight, flexible architecture** with **no vendor lock-in** and strong **AI model management** capabilities are particularly well positioned as buyers move away from monolithic, hardware-tied solutions toward intelligent, MSP-friendly systems.
